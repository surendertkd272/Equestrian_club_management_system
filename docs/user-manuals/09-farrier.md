# Farrier — User Guide
_Equiwings CMS · Log shoeing visits and keep horse feet on schedule_

## Logging in
Go to **/login** and sign in with the email and password your club gave you. On your first login you may be asked to set a new password. You land on your **Dashboard**, with the menu on the left. On a phone, tap the hamburger (☰) to open the menu.

## What you can do
Your main job here is **Farriery** — logging shoeing visits against each horse. You can view horse details, log any injuries or hoof problems you notice, complete tasks assigned to you, submit bills for your work or supplies, raise requisitions, and request leave.

## Your menu
**Overview**
- **Dashboard** — your daily snapshot.

**Staff & Operations**
- **Tasks** — the jobs assigned to you; tick them off as you complete them.
- **Leave Requests** — request your time off.
- **Requisitions** — request supplies you need.

**Stable & Veterinary**
- **Farriery** — your main screen: schedule and log shoeing visits per horse. *(if your club has this turned on)*
- **Horses** — view horse profiles and care details. *(if your club has this turned on)*
- **Injury Log** — log and track horse and rider injuries. *(if your club has this turned on)*

**Money & Records**
- **Submit Invoice** — drop in a bill for your work or materials (reimbursement).
- **Notifications** — your alerts.

## Common tasks
1. **Log a shoeing visit:** Open **Farriery**, pick the horse, and record the visit with the date and what you did.
2. **Check a horse before you start:** Open **Horses**, find the horse, and review its profile and care notes.
3. **Report a hoof or leg problem:** Open **Injury Log** and add an entry so the vet and stable team are alerted.
4. **Do an assigned task:** Open **Tasks**, see what's assigned to you, and mark it done when finished.
5. **Submit your bill:** Open **Submit Invoice**, enter the amount and details, and send it in for payment.
6. **Request supplies:** Open **Requisitions** and submit what you need.

## Your account & help
Use the **Account** area for your profile, password, notification preferences, and **Sign out everywhere**. The bell shows your **Notifications**, and the **Help** page is always available.

## Good to know
- Farriers are often first to catch a hoof issue — add it to the **Injury Log** so it doesn't get missed.
- On **Tasks** you complete jobs assigned to you; you won't be assigning tasks to others.
- A screen marked *(if your club has this turned on)* depends on your club's plan — if it's missing, it simply hasn't been enabled.
- It all works on a phone browser, so you can log visits on the move.
