# Coach — User Guide
_Equiwings CMS · Run your daily classes: lessons, attendance, progress, and updates._

## Logging in
Go to **/login** and sign in with the email and password your admin gave you. On your first login you may be asked to set a new password. You'll land on your **Dashboard** — a snapshot of your day, with the menu down the left side. On a phone the menu tucks into a hamburger (☰) at the top.

## What you can do
You're the hands-on trainer. You can:
- View your riders' profiles, levels and history (view-only — you can't edit core profile details)
- Create and manage your classes (Batches) and individual Lessons
- Mark attendance and track each rider's progress and monthly skills
- File your quick end-of-day Daily Coach Update and complete the Daily Checklist
- Log horse/rider injuries, request a one-day batch shift, complete tasks
- Submit invoices for things you paid for, raise requisitions, and request leave
- View reports

You do **not** manage staff, finances, or horse inventory — those belong to managers.

## Your menu
**Overview**
- **Dashboard** — your home snapshot for the day.

**Riders & Training**
- **Riders** — your rider roster: profiles, levels and history (view-only).
- **Batches** — your recurring class slots; create and manage your classes.
- **Lessons** — individual sessions; schedule, reschedule, cancel, complete, and assign horses.
- **Attendance** — mark riders present, absent or late _(if your club has this turned on)_.
- **Progress** — track each rider's skills over time _(if your club has this turned on)_.
- **Monthly Skills** — set this month's skill rating per rider: not-yet, needs-work, confident, or mastered.

**Staff & Operations**
- **Leave Requests** — request your time off _(if your club has this turned on)_.
- **Tasks** — your to-do board; complete items assigned to you _(if your club has this turned on)_.
- **Daily Checklist** — your daily operational checklist.
- **Daily Coach Update** — your 60-second end-of-day note.
- **Batch Shifts** — request a single-day change to your own batch (different time/day).
- **Requisitions** — request to purchase supplies.

**Stable & Veterinary**
- **Injury Log** — log a horse or rider injury _(if your club has this turned on)_.

**Money & Records**
- **Submit Invoice** — drop in bills you paid for the club, for reimbursement.
- **Reports** — monthly parent report cards and exports _(if your club has this turned on)_.
- **Notifications** — your alerts.

> Tip: your most-used screens (Lessons, Attendance, Progress, Daily Checklist) are also "Pinned for you" at the top of the menu.

## Common tasks
**1. Mark attendance for today's class**
1. Open **Attendance** (or tap a class from **Lessons**).
2. Pick the batch and date.
3. Tap each rider as present, absent or late, then save.

**2. Update a rider's progress**
1. Open **Progress** and choose the rider.
2. Log the skill or note from today's session and save.
3. For the month's overall rating, open **Monthly Skills** and set not-yet / needs-work / confident / mastered per rider.

**3. Schedule or complete a lesson**
1. Open **Lessons** and click to add a new session (or open an existing one).
2. Set the date, time and riders, and assign a horse.
3. After the session, open it and mark it **complete** — or reschedule/cancel if plans change.

**4. Create or manage a batch (your class)**
1. Open **Batches** and use the new-batch form.
2. Set the day, time, level and the coach, then save.
3. To remove one you created, use the delete control on its row.

**5. File your Daily Coach Update**
1. Open **Daily Coach Update** at end of day.
2. Write a quick summary, add how many horses/riders you worked with, and flag any injuries or concerns.
3. Save — it's one entry per day, and saving again just updates today's.

**6. Submit an invoice or request leave**
1. For a bill you paid: open **Submit Invoice**, enter the amount and details, attach the receipt, and submit.
2. For time off: open **Leave Requests**, pick your dates, add a reason, and submit for approval.

## Your account & help
- **Account** (/account) — update your profile, change your password, set notification preferences, and "Sign out everywhere".
- **Notifications** — tap the bell for alerts.
- **Help** (/help) — guidance and answers.

## Good to know
- Rider profiles are **view-only** for you — ask a manager to fix core details.
- A "(if your club has this turned on)" item is a plan feature; if you don't see it, your club hasn't enabled it.
- Batch Shifts are for a **one-day** change to your own batch — for a permanent change, talk to your manager.
- It all works on your phone browser, so you can mark attendance from the arena.
