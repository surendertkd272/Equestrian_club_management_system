# Veterinarian — User Guide
_Equiwings CMS · Keep every horse healthy: medicines, vaccinations, follow-ups, and injuries._

## Logging in
Go to **/login** and sign in with the email and password your admin gave you. On your first login you may be asked to set a new password. You land on your **Dashboard** with a left sidebar. On a phone, the menu tucks behind a hamburger icon at the top.

## What you can do
- Keep the **Vet Medicines** inventory accurate, and **prescribe** medicines for horses.
- Record **Vaccinations** and track which are due.
- Plan your week from the **Vet Follow-ups** calendar.
- Open any **Horse** profile to read and update its medical history.
- Track **First-Aid Consumables** stock.
- Log and follow horse and rider **Injuries**.
- **Submit invoices** for things you bought for the club, raise **Requisitions** for supplies, and request **Leave**.

## Your menu
**Overview**
- **Dashboard** — your home snapshot for the day.

**Staff & Operations**
- **Leave Requests** — request your time off (if your club has this turned on).
- **Tasks** — your to-do board; mark tasks complete.
- **Requisitions** — request to purchase supplies; track where each request is in approval.

**Stable & Veterinary** _(your core area)_
- **Vet Follow-ups** — calendar of upcoming follow-ups across all horses, grouped by overdue / this week / next 30 days.
- **Vet Medicines** — medicine inventory and stock; record stock in/out, usage, and expiry. You can also prescribe.
- **Horses** — the horse roster: profiles and full medical history.
- **Vaccinations** — vaccination records and due dates per horse.
- **First-Aid Consumables** — bandages, antiseptics and similar stock levels.
- **Injury Log** — log and track horse and rider injuries.

**Money & Records**
- **Submit Invoice** — drop in bills you paid for the club, for reimbursement.
- **Notifications** — your alerts.

(Items marked "if your club has this turned on" only appear when your club has the matching feature enabled.)

## Common tasks
1. **Start your day with follow-ups** — Open **Vet Follow-ups**. Anything in the "overdue" or "this week" group is a horse to see. Click through to the horse to act on it.
2. **Prescribe / record a medicine for a horse** — Open the horse in **Horses** (or go via **Vet Medicines**), record the medicine and dose; this updates the horse's medical history and draws down stock.
3. **Add a vaccination** — Open **Vaccinations**, add the record for the horse and vaccine, and set the next due date so it shows up as a reminder.
4. **Log an injury** — Open **Injury Log**, create an entry for the horse (or rider), describe it, and update it as the case progresses.
5. **Keep stock honest** — In **Vet Medicines** and **First-Aid Consumables**, record what you use so quantities and low-stock / expiry warnings stay accurate.
6. **Get reimbursed** — Bought something for the club? Use **Submit Invoice** to upload the bill. Need to order supplies? Use **Requisitions**.

## Your account & help
Open **Account** (/account) to edit your profile, change your password, set notification preferences, or **Sign out everywhere**. The **bell** at the top shows notifications. Stuck? Open **Help** (/help).

## Good to know
- You work within your assigned club; you see that club's horses and stock.
- A follow-up only appears on the calendar if you set its follow-up date when recording the visit — so always set the next date.
- Recording usage matters: it keeps expiry and low-stock alerts trustworthy.
- You can do all of this from a phone browser when you're out at the stables.
