# Parent — Your Guide
_Equiwings CMS · Follow your child's riding journey and handle fees with ease_

## Logging in
Open the app's login page and sign in with the email and password your club gave you. The first time, you may be asked to set a new password of your own. After that, you land on **"Your children"** — your home page. It works just as nicely on a phone as on a computer.

## What you'll see
**Your children** shows one card for each child linked to your account. Each card gives you the highlights at a glance:
- **Attendance (last 30 days)** — how often your child has been to class.
- **Upcoming exam** — the date of their next exam, if one is scheduled.
- **Latest certificate** — the most recent certificate they've earned.
- **Unpaid invoices** — how many fees are outstanding (this appears only if your club uses online payments).

Tap a child's name to open their **full profile**, with more detail: a longer attendance history, the skills they're working on, their exams and results, all their certificates, upcoming lessons, and — if your club uses payments — their invoices.

## What you can do
- Keep up with each child's attendance, skills, exams, and certificates.
- See upcoming lessons and exam dates.
- Pay club fees through the secure link the club sends you.

## Common tasks
1. **Check a child's attendance.** On **Your children**, look at the **Attendance (30d)** figure on their card, or open their full profile for the day-by-day history.
2. **Open a child's full profile.** Tap the child's name, or **Open full profile →**, to see everything in one place.
3. **Find the next exam or lesson.** It's on the child's card (**Upcoming exam**) and in more detail inside their full profile.
4. **View a certificate.** Open the child's full profile and scroll to **Certificates** to see each one your child has earned.
5. **Pay fees.** When fees are due, the club sends you a secure payment link by email or WhatsApp. Open the link, check the amount, and pay — no need to hunt for it inside the app.

## Your account & help
Use the **Account** area to update your details, change your password, set your notification preferences, or sign out. The **Help** page is there whenever you need it.

## Good to know
- If you see "No children linked yet," your account just needs to be connected to your child's profile — please contact your club and they'll link it for you.
- Have more than one child riding? They'll each get their own card on **Your children**.
- The **Unpaid invoices** figure and payment links only appear if your club uses online payments; otherwise you'll settle fees with the club directly.
