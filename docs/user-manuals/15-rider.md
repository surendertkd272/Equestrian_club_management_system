# Rider — Your Guide to the Student Portal
_Equiwings CMS · Your riding journey, all in one friendly place_

## Logging in
Open the app's login page and sign in with the email and password your club gave you. The first time, you might be asked to pick a new password — choose one you'll remember. Then you're in! You'll land on your own home page that says **"Hi <your name>! 🐎"**. It works great on a phone too, so you can check it on the go.

## What you'll see
Your home page is all about you and how you're doing:
- **My progress** — your current level, your batch, and how many skills you've mastered this month.
- **Attendance ring (last 90 days)** — a colourful circle showing how often you've made it to class.
- **My Class** — your batch name, the days you ride, and the time.
- **Request a batch shift** — ask to move your class (just one day, or permanently). Your coaches review and approve it.
- **Exam history** and **My Exams** — the exams you've taken, your level, the examiner, and your results.
- **Upcoming Lessons** — your next two weeks of lessons, including the horse you'll ride.
- **Recent Attendance** — colour-coded squares for your latest sessions.
- **This month's skills** — each skill your coach is tracking, rated **not yet**, **needs work**, **confident**, or **mastered**.
- **My Certificates** — certificates you've earned, with a link to verify them (great for sharing proof).

Sometimes a friendly **BMI heads-up** note may appear. A **payments** tile shows up only if your club turns it on — usually a parent handles fees through a link sent by email instead.

## What you can do
- See your progress, attendance, skills, exams, and certificates any time.
- Check which horse you'll ride at your upcoming lessons.
- Ask to shift your batch when you need to.

## Common tasks
1. **Check your attendance.** Look at the **Attendance** ring near the top — it shows your last 90 days at a glance.
2. **Find your next lesson and horse.** Scroll to **Upcoming Lessons** to see the date, time, batch, and the horse's name.
3. **Request a batch shift.** In **Request a batch shift**, pick the batch you'd like to move to and whether it's a single day or permanent, then send it. Your coach will approve or decline — you'll see the status right there.
4. **See this month's skills.** Scroll to **This month's skills** to see how your coach rated each one.
5. **View or share a certificate.** Open **My Certificates** and tap the verification link next to your latest one.

## Your account & help
Visit your **Account** area to update your profile, change your password, choose how you'd like to be notified, or sign out. The **Help** page is there whenever you have a question.

## Good to know
- If you see "Your Portal Isn't Set Up Yet," your account just needs to be linked to your rider profile — ask your club and they'll sort it quickly.
- Skill ratings come from your coach, so keep practising — they'll update as you improve!
- Haven't been put in a batch yet? Your **My Class** card will say so until your club assigns you one.
