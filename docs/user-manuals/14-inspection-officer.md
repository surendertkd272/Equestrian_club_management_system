# Inspection Officer — User Guide
_Equiwings CMS · Run clear, on-the-spot audits of a club's stores and stable_

## Logging in
Open the app's login page and sign in with the email and password the club gave you. On your first visit, you may be asked to set a new password. After login you land in the staff workspace — but don't worry about the long menus you might hear about; yours is deliberately simple. The app works fine on a phone, which is handy when you're walking the floor.

## Your menu
You have one job here, so you have one menu item: **Inspections**. That's where you start new audits and look back at past ones. You're tied to the club (centre) you've been assigned to, so everything you see and do stays within that club.

## What you can do
- **Start an inspection** and pick what you're auditing.
- **Walk the checklist**, marking each line **Pass**, **Fail**, or **N/A**, with remarks where useful.
- **Complete** the audit so it's saved as a finished record.
- **Revisit past audit runs** at any time to view what was found.

## Common tasks
1. **Start an audit.** Open **Inspections**, then in **Start an inspection** choose a scope:
   - **Tack & equipment inventory**
   - **Vet cabinet & medicines**
   - **Stable & feed store**
   - **Full audit (all areas)**

   Select **Start audit**. The app builds the right checklist for you automatically.
2. **Work through the sheet.** The checklist is grouped by area. For each line, tap **Pass**, **Fail**, or **N/A**.
3. **Add remarks.** When you mark a line **Fail** (or want to note something), a remarks box appears — type what's wrong or what action is needed.
4. **Complete the audit.** When you've marked every line, add an optional overall **Summary**, then choose **Complete audit**. A small note tells you how many lines are still pending, so nothing gets missed.
5. **Review a past audit.** Back on the **Inspections** page, the **Audit runs** list shows each run with its pass/fail/pending counts. Select **View** on a completed one (or **Continue** on one still in progress).

## Your account & help
Use the **Account** area to update your profile, change your password, set notification preferences, or sign out. The **Help** page is there whenever you need a hand.

## Good to know
- Once an audit is **Completed**, its lines are locked — you can still open and read it, but not change the marks. Finish a sheet only when you're confident it's done.
- A run you haven't finished stays available to **Continue** later, so you can pause and pick up where you left off.
- You can only start and view audits for your assigned club.
