# Super Admin — User Guide
_Equiwings CMS · the owner-level administrator with full access to everything across the whole organisation_

## Logging in
Go to your club's web address and open the login page. Sign in with the email and password your organisation set up for you. If it's your first time, you may be asked to set a new password. Once in, you land on the Dashboard with the left-hand menu. It works on your phone too — the menu tucks into a hamburger button.

## What you can do
- See and manage every club in the organisation from one place.
- Create and manage every login account, for every role, across all clubs.
- Compare clubs side by side and view organisation-wide billing and trends.
- Read the full Audit Log of who did what, anywhere in the system.
- Run and review inspections and SOP audits.
- Do anything any other role can do — riders, lessons, attendance, horses, staff, finance, events, the lot.
- Recover recently deleted catalog items from the Recycle Bin.

## Your menu
You see every section. The most important ones for your role:

**Overview**
- **Dashboard** — your home screen: today's classes, tasks, and alerts at a glance.
- **Analytics** — performance trend charts for riders, attendance, and revenue.
- **Clubs (HQ)** — the list of every club; create, edit, and manage them.
- **Users (HQ)** — manage all login accounts and roles across the organisation.
- **HQ Comparative** — side-by-side numbers for every centre.
- **HQ Invoices** — organisation-level billing across all clubs.

**Riders & Training** — Riders, Enrolment Approvals, Batches, Lessons, Attendance, Progress, Club Catalog, Monthly Skills, Exams.

**Staff & Operations** — Staff, Employee Onboarding, Staff Attendance, Gate Log, Training & Certs, Leave Requests, Tasks, Daily Checklist, Checklist Templates, Daily Coach Update, Team Daily Updates, Approvals, Batch Shifts, Requisitions, WhatsApp Links, Vendors, Salary Advances, Salary & Payroll, Facility Bookings.

**Stable & Veterinary** — Tack & Equipment, Vet Medicines, Vet Follow-ups, First-Aid Consumables, Horses, Vaccinations, Farriery, Injury Log.

**Events & Teams** — Events, Teams / Squads, Event Transport.

**Money & Records** — Finance, Reports, Procurement Report, Certificates, Accreditations, Notifications, **Audit Log** (only you see this), **Inspections**, **Recycle Bin**.

## Common tasks
1. **Add a new club** — Open **Clubs (HQ)**, choose to add a club, fill in its details, and save. It then appears in your comparative views and billing.
2. **Create a login for a new admin or manager** — Open **Users (HQ)**, add a user, pick their role and club, and save. They'll receive sign-in details to start.
3. **Compare how clubs are performing** — Open **HQ Comparative** for side-by-side figures, or **Analytics** for trend charts across riders, attendance, and revenue.
4. **Check who changed something** — Open the **Audit Log**, then search or filter by user, club, or date to trace any action in the system.
5. **Run an inspection** — Open **Inspections**, start an audit run, and mark each line item pass or fail as you go.
6. **Recover something deleted by mistake** — Open the **Recycle Bin**; deleted catalog items stay recoverable for 30 days before they're cleared automatically.

## Your account & help
- The **Notifications bell** (top bar) holds your alerts.
- The **Account** area lets you edit your profile, change your password, set notification preferences, and "Sign out everywhere" to log out of all devices.
- The **Help** page has guidance whenever you need it.

## Good to know
- You're the only role who can see the **Audit Log** and suspend a club, and only you manage organisation-wide user accounts.
- Some menu items only appear if your organisation has that feature turned on (for example Analytics, Events, or Certificates).
- You have full create and edit access everywhere — there's nothing in the app you can only view.
