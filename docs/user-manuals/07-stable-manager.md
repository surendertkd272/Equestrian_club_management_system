# Stable Manager — User Guide
_Equiwings CMS · Run the stable, grounds, and animal-care schedules_

## Logging in
Go to **/login** and sign in with the email and password your admin gave you. On your first login you may be asked to set a new password. You land on your **Dashboard** with the menu on the left. On a phone, tap the hamburger (☰) to open the menu.

## What you can do
You run the stable floor. You manage the horse roster, tack and grooming kit, first-aid stock, and the farrier/vet schedules. You can assign tasks to your team and complete your own, log injuries, run staff attendance and the gate kiosk, build event transport manifests, approve requests (including requisitions as a manager), submit invoices for things you bought, raise requisitions, and request leave.

## Your menu
**Overview**
- **Dashboard** — your daily snapshot.

**Staff & Operations**
- **Staff Attendance** — mark daily staff check-in.
- **Gate Log** — In/Out kiosk for people coming and going.
- **Leave Requests** — request your own time off.
- **Tasks** — to-do board; assign jobs to your team and tick off your own.
- **Daily Checklist** — complete the day's operational checklist.
- **Approvals** — approve or decline requests that need a manager.
- **Requisitions** — raise a purchase request, and approve your team's as manager.
- **WhatsApp Links** — generate WhatsApp contact links.

**Stable & Veterinary**
- **Tack & Equipment** — inventory of saddles, gear, and school equipment (stock levels). *(if your club has this turned on)*
- **Horses** — horse roster: profiles, stable info, medical, and care. *(if your club has this turned on)*
- **Farriery** — schedule and log farrier (shoeing) visits per horse. *(if your club has this turned on)*
- **Vaccinations** — view the horses' vaccination records. *(if your club has this turned on)*
- **Vet Follow-ups** — calendar of upcoming vet follow-ups. *(if your club has this turned on)*
- **First-Aid Consumables** — bandages and antiseptics stock. *(if your club has this turned on)*
- **Injury Log** — log and track horse and rider injuries. *(if your club has this turned on)*

**Events & Teams**
- **Event Transport** — horse and equipment manifest with check-out/check-in.

**Money & Records**
- **Submit Invoice** — drop in bills you paid for the club (reimbursement).
- **Notifications** — your alerts.

## Common tasks
1. **Add or update a horse:** Open **Horses**, pick a horse (or add a new one), and update its profile, stable details, medical notes, and care info.
2. **Schedule a farrier visit:** Open **Farriery**, choose the horse, and log the shoeing visit with the date and details.
3. **Check stock before you run low:** Open **Tack & Equipment** or **First-Aid Consumables** to see current stock; if you're short, head to **Requisitions** to request more.
4. **Assign a job to your team:** Open **Tasks**, create a task, and assign it to a staff member. Tick it complete when it's done.
5. **Approve a requisition:** Open **Approvals** (or **Requisitions**), review the request, and approve or decline it as manager.
6. **Set up event transport:** Open **Event Transport**, build the manifest of horses and equipment, then check items out before the trip and back in on return.

## Your account & help
Use the **Account** area for your profile, password, notification preferences, and **Sign out everywhere**. The bell icon shows **Notifications**. Visit **Help** for guidance.

## Good to know
- You see **Vaccinations** and **Vet Follow-ups** to stay on top of schedules, but the vet records the medicine and prescribing side.
- Grooms and farriers are often first to spot an injury — make sure it lands in the **Injury Log** so the vet sees it.
- Items marked *(if your club has this turned on)* depend on your club's plan; if you don't see one, your admin hasn't enabled it.
- Everything works on a phone browser, so you can update from the stable floor.
