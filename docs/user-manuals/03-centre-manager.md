# Centre Manager — User Guide
_Equiwings CMS · You run your club from here, end to end._

## Logging in
Go to **/login** and sign in with the email and password your admin gave you. On your first login you may be asked to set a new password. You'll land on your **Dashboard** — a live snapshot of your club, with the menu down the left. On a phone, the menu tucks into a hamburger (☰) at the top.

## What you can do
You own one club's day-to-day operations. You can create and edit almost everything at your centre — riders, batches, lessons, attendance, exams, staff accounts, horses, stable inventory, events, finance — and you clear the approval queues (enrolments, leave, requisitions, batch changes). You also issue certificates and run inspections.

## Your menu
**Overview**
- **Dashboard** — today's snapshot of your club.
- **Analytics** — performance trend charts (if your club has this turned on).

**Riders & Training**
- **Riders** — your rider roster: profiles, levels, contacts, parent links, history.
- **Enrolment Approvals** — approve or reject public sign-ups before they become registrations.
- **Batches** — recurring class slots (day, time, level, coach).
- **Lessons** — individual sessions; schedule, reschedule, cancel, complete, assign horses.
- **Attendance** — mark riders present, absent, or late.
- **Progress** — per-rider skill tracking.
- **Club Catalog** — fee plans, levels, and skills for your club.
- **Monthly Skills** — this month's skill ratings per rider.
- **Exams** — book exam days, follow the marking, correct results, and export them.

**Staff & Operations**
- **Staff** — staff directory; create accounts, set roles, KYC, salary band.
- **Employee Onboarding** — send self-registration links; approve or reject new joiners.
- **Staff Attendance** — daily staff check-in.
- **Gate Log** — in/out gate kiosk.
- **Training & Certs** — staff courses and certifications.
- **Leave Requests** — review and approve staff leave.
- **Tasks** — to-do board.
- **Daily Checklist** — the daily operational checklist.
- **Daily Coach Update** / **Team Daily Updates** — your end-of-day note and the team's rollup.
- **Approvals** — approve or decline requests.
- **Batch Shifts** — approve rider class-change requests.
- **Requisitions** — review and approve purchase requests.
- **WhatsApp Links** — generate WhatsApp message links.
- **Facility Bookings** — book arenas, wash bays, classrooms.

**Stable & Veterinary**
- **Tack & Equipment** — inventory.
- **Vet Medicines** — medicine stock.
- **Vet Follow-ups** — vet follow-up calendar.
- **First-Aid Consumables** — first-aid stock.
- **Horses** — horse roster and medical records.
- **Vaccinations** — horse vaccination records.
- **Farriery** — farrier visit scheduling and logs.
- **Injury Log** — horse and rider injuries.

**Events & Teams**
- **Events** — clinics, shows, schooling days.
- **Teams / Squads** — group riders into teams.
- **Event Transport** — transport manifest.

**Money & Records**
- **Finance** — your club's money dashboard.
- **Reports** — parent report cards and exports.
- **Procurement Report** — club spend snapshot.
- **Certificates** — issue QR-verified certificates.
- **Accreditations** — riders' federation memberships.
- **Notifications** — alerts.
- **Inspections** — run SOP, inventory, and stable audits.
- **Recycle Bin** — recover deleted items (within 30 days).

## Common tasks
1. **Approve a new sign-up:** Open **Enrolment Approvals**, review the applicant's details, then approve (they become a billable rider) or reject.
2. **Set up a class:** Go to **Batches**, create a slot (day, time, level, coach), then use **Lessons** to schedule the individual sessions and assign horses.
3. **Add a staff member:** Open **Employee Onboarding** to send a self-registration link, then approve them — or create the account directly in **Staff** and set their role, KYC, and salary band.
4. **Approve leave:** Open **Leave Requests**, review the dates, and approve or decline.
5. **Run an exam day:** In **Exams**, tap **Schedule exam day**. Name it, pick the date, give each rider their level (tap **Add all shown at their next level** to fill it in), and pick the examiners for each level. Riders still waiting for parental consent are listed separately with the reason — chase their consent first. The day page then shows every level's progress; you get one summary when each level finishes and one when the whole day is done.
   - **Visiting examiners:** judges from outside the club are registered under **Exams → Examiners → Register a visiting examiner** — name, email, and the date their access ends. Hand them the temporary password (Copy / WhatsApp); they set their own on first sign-in, and the Examiners page shows who has signed in and is ready. They only ever see the riders they examine, and their access ends automatically on the date you set (or **End access now**).
   - **Jury panels:** on an exam's page, add co-judges. The result waits until every judge has submitted, then uses the average. Remove a judge who didn't turn up and the exam finishes with the others.
   - **Changes:** move or cancel the whole day from its page, or remove one rider from a sitting. Riders who already have a result are never removed.
   - **Corrections:** on a completed exam, **Reopen for correction** and give a reason. The judges' cards unlock; when they re-submit, a pass keeps its certificate and a fail revokes it.
   - **Results:** **Export results** on Exams (or on the day or sitting) downloads a spreadsheet. Certificates for passes are issued automatically.
6. **Clear purchase requests:** Open **Requisitions** (or **Approvals**), check each request, and approve or decline.

## Your account & help
Open **Account** (/account) for your profile, password, notification preferences, and "Sign out everywhere." The bell at the top shows notifications. Need a hand? Visit **Help** (/help).

## Good to know
- Some menu items only appear if your club has that feature turned on (for example Analytics, Catalog, Events, Certificates).
- Deleted something by mistake? Check the **Recycle Bin** — items are recoverable for 30 days, then purged automatically.
- You see and manage data for your own club only.
- The menu works on a phone browser; tap the hamburger (☰) to open it.
