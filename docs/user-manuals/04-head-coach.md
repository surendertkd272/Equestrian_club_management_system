# Head Coach — User Guide
_Equiwings CMS · Lead the coaching team and keep training on track._

## Logging in
Go to **/login** and sign in with the email and password your admin gave you. On your first login you may be asked to set a new password. You'll land on your **Dashboard**, with the menu down the left. On a phone, the menu tucks into a hamburger (☰) at the top.

## What you can do
You lead the coaching team. You manage riders, batches, lessons, attendance, progress, monthly skills, exams (you can both schedule and score), teams, and events. You approve your team's leave, requisitions, and batch-change requests, write daily updates and see the team rollup, manage horses, log injuries, and book facilities. You can submit invoices for things you bought for the club. You do **not** manage staff accounts or run finance/payroll — those stay with the Centre Manager and Accountant.

## Your menu
**Overview**
- **Dashboard** — today's snapshot of the club.
- **Analytics** — performance trend charts (if your club has this turned on).

**Riders & Training**
- **Riders** — rider roster: profiles, levels, contacts, parent links, history.
- **Batches** — recurring class slots (day, time, level, coach).
- **Lessons** — individual sessions; schedule, reschedule, cancel, complete, assign horses.
- **Attendance** — mark riders present, absent, or late.
- **Progress** — per-rider skill tracking.
- **Monthly Skills** — this month's skill ratings per rider.
- **Exams** — schedule, score, and record exam results and levels.

**Staff & Operations**
- **Staff Attendance** — daily staff check-in.
- **Gate Log** — in/out gate kiosk.
- **Training & Certs** — staff courses and certifications.
- **Leave Requests** — request leave, and approve your team's leave.
- **Tasks** — to-do board.
- **Daily Checklist** — the daily operational checklist.
- **Daily Coach Update** — your quick end-of-day note.
- **Team Daily Updates** — rollup of your team's daily updates.
- **Approvals** — approve or decline requests.
- **Batch Shifts** — approve rider class-change requests.
- **Requisitions** — submit purchase requests, and approve your team's.
- **WhatsApp Links** — generate WhatsApp message links.
- **Facility Bookings** — book arenas, wash bays, classrooms.

**Stable & Veterinary**
- **Tack & Equipment** — inventory.
- **Vet Follow-ups** — vet follow-up calendar.
- **Horses** — horse roster and medical records.
- **Injury Log** — horse and rider injuries.

**Events & Teams**
- **Events** — clinics, shows, schooling days.
- **Teams / Squads** — group riders into teams.
- **Event Transport** — transport manifest.

**Money & Records**
- **Submit Invoice** — drop in bills you paid for the club.
- **Reports** — parent report cards and exports.
- **Accreditations** — riders' federation memberships.
- **Notifications** — alerts.

## Common tasks
1. **Plan the week's classes:** In **Batches**, set up the recurring slots (day, time, level, coach), then use **Lessons** to schedule sessions and assign horses.
2. **Track a rider's growth:** Mark **Attendance**, log skills in **Progress**, and set this month's ratings in **Monthly Skills**.
3. **Run an exam:** In **Exams**, schedule it, then record scores and publish results and levels.
4. **Approve your team's requests:** Open **Approvals**, **Leave Requests**, **Batch Shifts**, or **Requisitions** and approve or decline.
5. **Wrap up the day:** Write your **Daily Coach Update**, then review **Team Daily Updates** to see what the rest of the coaching team logged.
6. **Send in a bill:** Use **Submit Invoice** to record something you purchased for the club, with the amount and a receipt.

## Your account & help
Open **Account** (/account) for your profile, password, notification preferences, and "Sign out everywhere." The bell at the top shows notifications. Need a hand? Visit **Help** (/help).

## Good to know
- Some menu items only appear if your club has that feature turned on (for example Analytics, Events, Teams).
- You can submit invoices and requisitions, but the full **Finance** dashboard and payroll stay with the Centre Manager and Accountant.
- Staff accounts (creating logins, KYC, salary) are managed by the Centre Manager — you focus on the coaching side.
- The menu works on a phone browser; tap the hamburger (☰) to open it.
