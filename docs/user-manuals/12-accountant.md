# Accountant — User Guide
_Equiwings CMS · The club's money, clear and in balance._

## Logging in
Go to **/login** and sign in with the email and password your admin gave you. Your first login may prompt you to set a new password. You land on your **Dashboard** with a left sidebar. On a phone, the menu collapses into a hamburger icon.

## What you can do
- Run the **Finance** dashboard — view income, expenses and profit/loss, **and record payments** (you have full read **and** write on finance).
- Run monthly **Salary & Payroll**, which auto-deducts outstanding advances.
- Record and track **Salary Advances** paid to staff and their repayments.
- Read the **Procurement Report** (club-wise spend on farrier / fodder / hay / medicines).
- See **Analytics** performance and financial trend charts.
- View **Vet Medicines** for cost/stock context.
- Approve requests in your **Approvals** queue, and give **accountant approval** on **Requisitions**.
- **Submit invoices** for club purchases and request **Leave**.

## Your menu
**Overview**
- **Dashboard** — your home snapshot.
- **Analytics** — performance and financial trend charts (if your club has this turned on).

**Staff & Operations**
- **Leave Requests** — request your time off (if your club has this turned on).
- **Tasks** — your to-do board.
- **Approvals** — review and approve or decline requests routed to you (if your club has this turned on).
- **Requisitions** — submit your own requests, and give the accountant-stage approval on requests waiting for finance sign-off.
- **Salary Advances** — record advances paid to staff and track repayments.
- **Salary & Payroll** — run monthly payroll.

**Stable & Veterinary**
- **Vet Medicines** — medicine inventory and stock, for cost and spend context (if your club has this turned on).

**Money & Records** _(your core area)_
- **Finance** — money dashboard: income, expenses, P&L; record cash payments, manage invoices, and export.
- **Submit Invoice** — drop in bills you paid for the club, for reimbursement.
- **Procurement Report** — club-wise snapshot of the most recent purchase in each category: farrier, fodder, hay, vet medicines (if your club has this turned on).
- **Notifications** — your alerts.

(Items marked "if your club has this turned on" only appear when your club has the matching feature enabled.)

## Common tasks
1. **Check the money picture** — Open **Finance** to see income, receivables, expenses and overdue invoices for the period.
2. **Record a payment** — In **Finance**, use **Record Payment** to log a cash or offline payment against an invoice; the totals update straight away.
3. **Run monthly payroll** — Open **Salary & Payroll** and record the month. Net pay is computed automatically: base, minus absent-day deductions, minus any advance recovery and other deductions.
4. **Issue or settle a salary advance** — Open **Salary Advances**, issue a new advance to a staff member, or record a repayment. Outstanding advances are auto-recovered at payroll time.
5. **Approve a requisition** — Open **Requisitions**; the accountant queue lists requests waiting for finance sign-off. Approve or decline each one.
6. **Review spend** — Open the **Procurement Report** to compare each club's latest purchase rate, quantity, amount and vendor by category.

## Your account & help
Open **Account** (/account) for your profile, password, notification preferences, or **Sign out everywhere**. The **bell** at the top shows notifications. Need help? Open **Help** (/help).

## Good to know
- You have full finance read **and** write — you can record payments and manage invoices, not just look.
- Requisitions can pass through two approval stages: a manager approves first, then the accountant (you) gives the finance sign-off.
- Payroll and advances are linked: any outstanding advance is deducted automatically when you run that person's salary.
- Figures are scoped to your assigned club. Everything works on a phone browser too.
