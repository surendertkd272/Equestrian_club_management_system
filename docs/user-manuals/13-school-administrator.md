# School Administrator — User Guide
_Equiwings CMS · A clear, read-only window into your students' riding journey_

## Logging in
Open the app's login page and sign in with the email and password the club gave you. If the club issued you a temporary password, you'll be asked to choose your own before you can go any further — that's expected, and it only happens once. After that you land straight on your school dashboard. It works just as well in a phone browser as on a computer.

## Finding your way around
Across the top of the portal there are five tabs:

- **Overview** — the summary screen you land on.
- **Riders** — your full student roll.
- **Horse Workload** — how hard the club's horses are working.
- **Exams & Leaderboard** — results, and where your students placed.
- **Tasks** — your direct line to the club's head office.

Next to the sign-out button you'll also find **Account** and **Help**.

This is an oversight view. You can read everything shown to you, but you can't change rider details or run day-to-day club operations — the club's staff handle that. The one exception is approving sign-ups, below.

## Overview
Your dashboard, from top to bottom:

- **Self-enrolments awaiting your approval** — appears only when someone has signed up through the club's public link. The club checks their documents first; once that's done, **Approve** becomes available to you.
- **Cannot ride yet — consent not signed** — students added from a spreadsheet, which can't carry a signature. Until a parent signs the indemnity and injury NOC, these children can't be put on a register. Each one goes active automatically the moment it's signed. The club emails parents a signing link, and a nudge from the school usually moves it along faster.
- **Riders** — every active student, with their school, level, joining date, and attendance this month.
- **Recent exams (last 60 days)** and **Skill progress** — who tested and what the coaches have logged lately.

## Riders
The full roll, with more detail than the dashboard: class and section, age, level, batch and coach, height/weight/BMI, allergies, joining date, attendance this month, and whether the student is cleared to ride or still waiting on consent.

Attendance reads as **turned up / sessions offered** — so 6/8 means six attended out of eight classes held. Absences are not counted as attendance.

Class and measurements are what your school supplied; level, batch and attendance come from the club.

Some things are deliberately not here: Aadhaar numbers, fees and invoices, and medical notes. A partner school isn't the child's guardian and has no part in the club's billing. Allergies are the one medical detail included, because a school taking children to a stable needs it.

## Horse Workload
How much work each of the club's horses has done over the last 30 days: sessions, total hours, hours per week, and how much of that was ridden by **your** students.

If a horse is averaging more than 18 hours a week, it's flagged at the top. Treat that as a good reason to ask the club how the rota is being managed — not as a welfare finding on its own. A horse doing short, light lessons isn't the same as one jumping all day.

Note that only booked sessions count — lessons, exams and competitions. Hacking out or turnout that nobody booked doesn't appear, so read these numbers as a floor rather than a total.

## Exams & Leaderboard
Upcoming exams first, then a leaderboard for each level your students have sat.

Only **your** students are named. Each one also carries their true placing against everyone who sat that level at the club — "2nd of 14" — so the comparison is honest while other schools' children stay anonymous.

## Tasks
Your thread with the club's head office. Raise a request, track it, and close it when it's settled. You'll see what you raised and anything head office has addressed to you — never the club's internal work. If the **Tasks** tab shows a number, that's how many items are waiting on you.

You can't assign work to the club's coaches or grooms, by design; a request goes to head office and they decide who picks it up.

## Common tasks
1. **Check this month's attendance.** Open **Riders** — the attendance column shows turned up / sessions offered for each student.
2. **Approve a new sign-up.** When the amber card appears on your **Overview**, check the name, mobile and school, then choose **Approve**. If Approve isn't available yet, the club hasn't finished checking that person's documents.
3. **Reject a sign-up.** Same card, choose **Reject** — for a duplicate or a test entry, for example.
4. **Chase a consent form.** The rose-coloured card on your **Overview** lists every student who can't ride yet. A word from the school to those parents is usually what moves it.
5. **See how your students placed.** Open **Exams & Leaderboard** and look at the level you're interested in.
6. **Ask the club for something.** Open **Tasks** and raise it there, so it's on record and someone at head office picks it up.

## Your account & help
**Account**, in the top-right, is where you update your profile, change your password, and set how you'd like to be notified. **Help** has guidance on every part of the portal. Your club contact is always happy to assist too.

## Good to know
- If your account is attached to a specific school, everything in the portal shows only that school's children. If your club serves just one school, you'll see all of its riders — same thing.
- If you see "No club assigned," your account hasn't been linked to a club yet — ask the club's admin to connect you.
- Cards that have nothing in them don't appear at all. No approval card simply means nothing is waiting on you.
- If a banner says the account is read-only, the club's billing needs attention — you can still read everything, but approvals are paused until it's sorted.
- Everything reflects the latest information from the club, refreshed each time you open a page.
