# School Administrator — User Guide
_Equiwings CMS · A clear, read-only window into your students' riding journey_

## Logging in
Open the app's login page and sign in with the email and password the club gave you. The very first time, you may be asked to choose a new password of your own. Once you're in, you land straight on your school dashboard — there's nothing to set up. It works just as well in a phone browser as on a computer.

## What you'll see
Your dashboard is a calm, read-only snapshot of the riders at your club. From top to bottom:
- **Self-enrolments awaiting your approval** — appears only when someone has signed up through the club's public link. This is the one place you can take action (see below).
- **Riders** — every active rider, with their school, current level, joining date, and how many sessions they've attended this month.
- **Recent exams (last 60 days)** — who tested, at what level, and the result.
- **Skill progress** — the latest skill updates the coaches have logged for your riders.

This is an oversight view. You can read everything, but you can't change rider details or run day-to-day club operations — the club's staff handle that.

## What you can do
- Keep an eye on attendance, levels, exams, and skill progress for all your riders.
- **Approve or reject** new riders who signed up through the public link, so only genuine sign-ups become registered students.

## Common tasks
1. **Check this month's attendance.** Open your dashboard and look at the **Riders** table — the right-hand column shows sessions attended this month for each rider.
2. **See who recently took an exam.** Scroll to **Recent exams (last 60 days)** to view each rider's level and whether they passed.
3. **Review skill progress.** Scroll to **Skill progress** for the most recent coach updates on each rider's skills.
4. **Approve a new sign-up.** When the amber **Self-enrolments awaiting your approval** card appears, check the person's name, mobile, and school, then choose **Approve** to start their registration.
5. **Reject a sign-up.** In that same card, choose **Reject** for any entry that doesn't belong (for example, a duplicate or test sign-up).

## Your account & help
Visit your **Account** area any time to update your profile, change your password, set how you'd like to be notified, or sign out. If you ever get stuck, the **Help** page has guidance, and your club contact is always happy to assist.

## Good to know
- If you see "No club assigned," your account hasn't been linked to a club yet — just ask the club's admin to connect you.
- The approval card only shows up when there are pending sign-ups; no card simply means there's nothing waiting on you.
- Everything you see reflects the latest information from the club, refreshed each time you open the page.
