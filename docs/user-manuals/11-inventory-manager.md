# Inventory Manager — User Guide
_Equiwings CMS · Keep the shelves stocked and the counts accurate._

## Logging in
Go to **/login** and sign in with the email and password your admin gave you. Your first login may ask you to set a new password. You land on your **Dashboard** with a left sidebar. On a phone, the menu collapses into a hamburger icon.

## What you can do
- Own the **Tack & Equipment** inventory: stock levels and reorder.
- Manage the **stock side** of **Vet Medicines** (quantities, restock, expiry) — note: you do **not** prescribe; the vet does that.
- Keep **First-Aid Consumables** topped up.
- Clear items in your **Approvals** queue.
- Work your **Tasks** board.
- Raise and track **Requisitions** to purchase supplies.
- **Submit invoices** for purchases you made for the club, and request **Leave**.

## Your menu
**Overview**
- **Dashboard** — your home snapshot.

**Staff & Operations**
- **Leave Requests** — request your time off (if your club has this turned on).
- **Tasks** — your to-do board; mark tasks complete.
- **Approvals** — review and approve or decline requests routed to you (if your club has this turned on).
- **Requisitions** — request to purchase supplies and track each request's approval stage.

**Stable & Veterinary** _(your core area)_
- **Tack & Equipment** — inventory of gear and school equipment, with stock levels and reorder cues (if your club has this turned on).
- **Vet Medicines** — medicine inventory and stock; record restock and quantities (you manage stock, not prescriptions) (if your club has this turned on).
- **First-Aid Consumables** — bandages, antiseptics and similar; keep counts current (if your club has this turned on).

**Money & Records**
- **Submit Invoice** — drop in bills you paid for the club, for reimbursement.
- **Notifications** — your alerts.

(Items marked "if your club has this turned on" only appear when your club has the matching feature enabled.)

## Common tasks
1. **Do a stock check** — Open **Tack & Equipment** to scan quantities against reorder points; repeat for **Vet Medicines** and **First-Aid Consumables**. Anything flagged low (or expiring) is your reorder list.
2. **Record a restock** — When new stock arrives, open the right inventory page and update the quantity so the counts match the shelf.
3. **Raise a purchase request** — Open **Requisitions**, add the items, and submit. Your request flows to a manager (and, where needed, the accountant) for approval — you can watch its stage on the same page.
4. **Clear your Approvals** — Open **Approvals** and act on anything waiting for you, so requests don't stall.
5. **Get reimbursed** — Bought supplies yourself? Use **Submit Invoice** to upload the bill.
6. **Stay on top of tasks** — Check **Tasks** and mark items done as you complete them.

## Your account & help
Open **Account** (/account) for your profile, password, notification preferences, or **Sign out everywhere**. The **bell** at the top shows notifications. Need a hand? Open **Help** (/help).

## Good to know
- You manage stock quantities for medicines, but prescribing stays with the vet — you won't see prescribe controls.
- Keeping quantities current is what makes low-stock and expiry alerts reliable, so record every in and out.
- You work within your assigned club; your counts and requests are scoped to it.
- Everything works on a phone browser for quick counts on the floor.
