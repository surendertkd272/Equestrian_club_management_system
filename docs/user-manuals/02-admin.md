# Admin — User Guide
_Equiwings CMS · an HQ-level administrator who runs day-to-day operations and finance across all clubs_

## Logging in
Go to your club's web address and open the login page. Sign in with the email and password your organisation set up for you. If it's your first time, you may be asked to set a new password. Once in, you land on the Dashboard with the left-hand menu. It works on your phone too — the menu tucks into a hamburger button.

## What you can do
- Get an organisation-wide view: compare clubs, see overall billing, and watch trends.
- Browse the list of all clubs and their details.
- Manage staff: create staff accounts, send onboarding links, and approve new hires.
- Maintain each club's fee plans, levels, and skills, and set up checklist templates.
- Approve rider batch-change requests and review requisitions.
- Run the money side: Finance, monthly payroll, salary advances, and vendor contacts.
- Issue certificates, track federation accreditations, and pull reports.
- Run inspections and recover deleted catalog items.

## Your menu

**Overview**
- **Dashboard** — your home screen: a snapshot of activity, tasks, and alerts.
- **Analytics** — performance trend charts for riders, attendance, and revenue.
- **Clubs (HQ)** — the list of every club, with their details.
- **Users (HQ)** — view login accounts across the organisation.
- **HQ Comparative** — side-by-side numbers for every centre.
- **HQ Invoices** — organisation-level billing across all clubs.

**Riders & Training**
- **Enrolment Approvals** — approve or reject public sign-ups before they become registrations.
- **Batches** — recurring class slots (day, time, level, coach).
- **Club Catalog** — each club's fee plans, levels, and skills.
- **Monthly Skills** — this month's skill ratings per rider.
- **Exams** — schedule formal exams, record results, and set levels.

**Staff & Operations**
- **Staff** — the staff directory; create accounts, set roles, KYC, and salary band.
- **Employee Onboarding** — send self-registration links to new hires and approve them.
- **Training & Certs** — staff courses and external certifications.
- **Leave Requests** — request time off and approve others' leave.
- **Tasks** — the to-do board for daily centre tasks.
- **Daily Checklist** — the daily operational checklist.
- **Checklist Templates** — define the checklist items teams complete.
- **Daily Coach Update** — quick end-of-day coach notes.
- **Team Daily Updates** — the rollup of the team's daily updates.
- **Batch Shifts** — review and approve rider requests to change class.
- **Requisitions** — request to purchase supplies, and approve them.
- **WhatsApp Links** — generate WhatsApp message links to contact riders and parents.
- **Vendors** — your supplier contact database.
- **Salary Advances** — record and track advances paid to staff.
- **Salary & Payroll** — run monthly payroll (it auto-deducts outstanding advances).

**Events & Teams**
- **Event Transport** — the horse and equipment transport manifest, with check-out and check-in.

**Money & Records**
- **Finance** — the money dashboard: invoices, payments, recording cash, and exports.
- **Reports** — monthly parent report cards and exports.
- **Procurement Report** — a club-wise spend snapshot (farrier, fodder, hay, medicines).
- **Certificates** — issue QR-verified certificates.
- **Accreditations** — track riders' federation memberships (EFI/BHS/FEI).
- **Notifications** — your alerts inbox.
- **Inspections** — run SOP, inventory, and stable audits, marking each line pass or fail.
- **Recycle Bin** — recover deleted catalog items (kept 30 days).

## Common tasks
1. **Onboard a new staff member** — Open **Employee Onboarding** and send a self-registration link; when they finish, approve them. To add an account directly, use **Staff**.
2. **Run monthly payroll** — Open **Salary & Payroll** and run the month; outstanding **Salary Advances** are deducted automatically. Record advances first under **Salary Advances** if needed.
3. **Approve a class change** — Open **Batch Shifts**, review each rider's request, and approve or decline.
4. **Set up a club's fee plans and levels** — Open **Club Catalog**, pick the club, and edit its plans, levels, and skills.
5. **Issue a certificate** — Open **Certificates**, choose the rider and template, and issue it; the certificate carries a QR code for verification.
6. **Compare clubs** — Use **HQ Comparative** for side-by-side figures and **HQ Invoices** for billing across all clubs.

## Your account & help
- The **Notifications bell** (top bar) holds your alerts.
- The **Account** area lets you edit your profile, change your password, set notification preferences, and "Sign out everywhere" to log out of all devices.
- The **Help** page has guidance whenever you need it.

## Good to know
- A few areas aren't on your menu: rider rosters, lessons, attendance, progress, horses, vaccinations, farriery, and the events/teams setup are run by centre staff. If you need something there, ask the Centre Manager or Super Admin.
- You can't manage organisation-wide login accounts, view the Audit Log, or suspend a club — those stay with the Super Admin.
- Some items only appear if your organisation has that feature turned on (for example Analytics, HQ Comparative, Training & Certs, Certificates, or Accreditations).
