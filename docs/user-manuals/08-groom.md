# Groom — User Guide
_Equiwings CMS · Hands-on daily horse care_

## Logging in
Go to **/login** and sign in with the email and password your manager gave you. The first time, you may be asked to pick a new password. You land on your **Dashboard**. The menu is on the left — on a phone, tap the hamburger (☰) to open it.

## What you can do
Your day is hands-on with the horses. You work through your **Daily Checklist** and **Tasks**, look up horse details, keep an eye on tack and first-aid stock, log any injuries you spot, submit bills for things you bought, request supplies, and ask for leave.

## Your menu
**Overview**
- **Dashboard** — your home snapshot for the day.

**Staff & Operations**
- **Tasks** — your to-do board; the daily jobs assigned to you.
- **Daily Checklist** — the day's care checklist to complete.
- **Leave Requests** — ask for time off.
- **Requisitions** — request supplies you need.

**Stable & Veterinary**
- **Tack & Equipment** — saddles, gear, and stock you use each day. *(if your club has this turned on)*
- **First-Aid Consumables** — bandages and antiseptics stock. *(if your club has this turned on)*
- **Horses** — horse profiles, stable info, and care notes. *(if your club has this turned on)*
- **Injury Log** — log and track horse and rider injuries. *(if your club has this turned on)*

**Money & Records**
- **Submit Invoice** — drop in a bill you paid for the club to get paid back.
- **Notifications** — your alerts.

## Common tasks
1. **Start your day:** Open **Daily Checklist** and work down the list, ticking off each item as you finish it.
2. **Do your assigned jobs:** Open **Tasks** to see what's been given to you, and mark each one done as you go.
3. **Look up a horse:** Open **Horses**, find the horse, and check its care notes and details.
4. **Report an injury you spot:** Open **Injury Log** and add a new entry with what you saw — the vet and your manager will see it.
5. **Ask for supplies:** Open **Requisitions** and submit a request for what you need.
6. **Get paid back for a purchase:** Open **Submit Invoice**, add the bill and amount, and send it in.

## Your account & help
Use the **Account** area for your profile, password, notification settings, and **Sign out everywhere**. The bell shows your **Notifications**. The **Help** page is there if you get stuck.

## Good to know
- Grooms are often the first to notice when a horse isn't right — when in doubt, add it to the **Injury Log**.
- If a screen marked *(if your club has this turned on)* isn't there, your club just hasn't switched it on — that's normal.
- You can do all of this from your phone, right in the stable.
